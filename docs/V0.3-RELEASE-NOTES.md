# v0.3 — controls, perspective, and localization

## Changes

- Regenerated buildings, tree crowns, rocks and scenery from an overhead viewpoint.
- Front/back walking artwork for all three heroes; horizontal controls unchanged.
- French and Arabic, including progressive intro subtitles, skills, menus and HUD. Arabic uses a bundled font and RTL layout. Change language from the top-right menu selector; selection persists locally.
- Familiar menu terms: Play, Upgrades, Shop, Game Mode, Start Run, Paused, Game Over, Victory. Skill descriptions state their implemented mechanics.
- Auto-attack / Manual attack in Settings and hero selection. Hold F or left mouse on PC; hold Attack on touch. Mouse attacks aim at the clicked arena location. F/touch uses the selected aim-assist behavior.
- Normal enemies and bosses spawn outside the camera edges. Ground-only Balam never receives bats; his flying bosses become Jaguar Chieftain / High Priest encounters.
- At level 5, a teammate joins for the rest of the run. Kukul supports Balam/Ixchel; Ixchel supports Kukul. They follow, face their movement direction, and shoot nearby enemies every 1.2 seconds. Damage scales modestly with level. Stronger enemies enter the spawn pool at the same milestone.
- New jaguar logo in the main menu and Android launcher, plus a separate Play Store listing PNG.

## QA and boundaries

Passed: 12 unit/asset tests, 23 baseline browser checks, 8 extended combat checks, 49 new browser checks, 8 baseline Android checks, 9 new Android checks. The extended test executes all 60 skills; it is not a full 20-minute balance study. Visual checks cover desktop, portrait and landscape viewports plus an Android emulator. French/Arabic should receive native-speaker playtesting before a commercial launch.

The APK is debug-signed for preview/sideload testing, not a production-signed Play Store submission. Monetization is unchanged: the shop previews planned cosmetics, with no live checkout or paid stat boosts. The separately packaged art/music download can be sold through itch.io.

Builds are in `release/`; source artwork and exact generation prompts remain in the project. Previous archives are retained.
