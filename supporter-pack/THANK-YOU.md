# Thank you for supporting Sangre y Jade

This v0.2 supporter archive contains:

- five remastered music loops and a dedicated 27-second prologue score, as stereo WAV files;
- thirteen game sound effects;
- the original pixel-art title illustration and six-panel story atlas;
- the art and asset provenance notes;
- the Version 0 design and balance codex.

Your support funds additional champions, environments, enemy animations, music, localization, cultural consultation, and the future Lords of Xibalba expansion.

These files are for personal enjoyment. Please do not redistribute the archive or sell the included assets separately.
